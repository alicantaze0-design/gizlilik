## 4. Sahne prompt'ları (Flow · Nano Banana · Resim · 9:16 · x1)

**Sahne 1 · Kapak ve kanca**
```
Low-angle dramatic view of a sharp pointed rocky peak rising above a deep green river valley, ruins of a Hellenistic stone fortress on its summit with thin smoke rising into a dusky orange sky. Hazelnut orchards and a winding modern road at the bottom. In the foreground a stick-figure narrator in a green jacket stands on the road looking up with a surprised expression.
```

**Sahne 2 · Mithridates'in kalesi**
```
A stick-figure Pontic king with a small golden diadem and long purple cloak stands on the fortress wall atop the rocky peak, pointing down at a winding river valley. Banners with an eight-rayed star and crescent emblem flutter on the towers. The Black Sea glimmers far on the horizon. Two stick-figure guards with round bronze shields stand beside him.
```

**Sahne 3 · Kuşatmaya hazırlık**
```
Cutaway view inside the rock: a long staircase carved directly into the stone descends deep into the mountain toward an underground water cistern. Next to it a storage room filled with rows of large clay pithos jars. Stick-figure soldiers in ochre tunics carry grain sacks and water jars down the steps. Torchlight, warm shadows.
```

**Sahne 4 · Roma geliyor**
```
Siege scene at the fortress walls: stick-figure Roman legionaries in red tunics, metal helmets and curved rectangular shields climb the rocky slope and hurl long iron-shanked javelins (pila). One legionary uses a long pole with a curved hooked blade to pull stones off the battlements. Arrows in the air, flames starting on a wooden tower. Dramatic, dark sky.
```

**Sahne 5 · Yangından sonra**
```
The burned fortress at night after the battle: charred wooden beams, collapsed walls, broken clay pithos jars spilling grain, a single Roman javelin stuck in the ash. Silent and empty, moonlight, wisps of smoke. Grass and moss beginning to creep over the stones.
```

**Sahne 6 · Kybele**
```
Modern archaeological excavation trench on the rocky summit with string grid lines and labelled buckets. Two stick-figure archaeologists in sun hats kneel with trowels and brushes, uncovering a seated marble mother-goddess statue. A small bronze coin rests on the goddess's lap and catches a soft beam of light. One archaeologist has an amazed expression.
```

**Sahne 7 · Bugün**
```
Present-day view of the excavation site on top of the pointed rock: wooden walkways, white tents, parts of ancient walls exposed while large areas are still covered in earth and grass. A few stick-figure visitors look around. Far in the distance the city of Ordu and the Black Sea under a clear afternoon sky.
```

**Sahne 8 · Soru**
```
Close-up of the seated marble goddess statue's lap with the small ancient bronze coin. The stick-figure narrator from scene 1 leans in from the side, chin on hand, curious thoughtful expression. Warm soft light, shallow depth, calm background.
```

## 5. Arşiv kartı (ordulog'un "polaroid" tekniği)
- Sahne 6'nın üstüne çerçeveli gerçek Kybele fotoğrafı: alt etiket "2016 · Kurul Kalesi kazısı: Kybele heykeli".
- Haber ajansı fotoğrafları telifli. Fotoğrafı kazı heyetinden, Ordu Valiliği'nden ya da Ordu Büyükşehir'den izinle alın veya müzede kendiniz çekin. İzin gelene kadar illüstrasyon tek başına yeterli.

- Seslendirme −14 LUFS, müzik altında −28 civarı.
